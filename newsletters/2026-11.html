Project Updates
The MB5N (Naturalistic) spin-off study is currently in the preparation phase for data collection. This add-on study to MB5 combines survey-based data collection and in-lab coding of naturalistic play sessions to examine relationships between caregiver and infant behaviours related to novelty-seeking, as well as the relationships between infants’ naturalistic and experimental novelty behaviours. We offer a range of data contribution options to accommodate different lab resources, interests, and data-sharing capabilities. These include a survey-only option (in addition to participation in the main MB5 study) and several options involving brief video-recorded naturalistic play sessions. We are currently preparing for data collection and welcoming contributions from interested labs across the ManyBabies community. For details about the study and participation options, please refer to the MB5N manual.
Now that the MB6 Stage 1 Registered Report has received "In-Principle Acceptance" from Developmental Science, stay tuned for news on next steps as we prepare for data collection. If you are interested in collecting data for the project in your lab, please indicate your interest using the LabID Form.
An integrated draft of the introduction is close to being sent around for feedback. Design subgroups continue to work out data simulation, stimulus sets, and additional components.
The initial meeting for Data Collection sites was held last week with an updated timeline and plan for data collection. Preparation of materials for data collection are now underway (e.g., ethics templates, translations/adaptations of measures to each language, recruitment protocols) to be shared with sites by the end of the year.
MBAH1-LWL (looking-while-listening): The Stage 1 revision has been submitted! Two more languages, Akan and Tagalog, have now been added to the confirmed languages list, bringing the total number of confirmed languages to be tested to eleven! We will open the call for participation once the Stage I revision is accepted.
