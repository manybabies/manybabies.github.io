Project Updates
MB2 (Knowledge-Ignorance in Toddlers): The Stage 2 Registered Report has been submitted and is under review!

MB2.2 (Knowledge-Ignorance in Toddlers Adaption): Currently being designed by a task force.

MB2.3 (False Belief in Adults): Call for Data Collection! See the MB2.3 Lab Manual for more information and sign up here. Contact manybabies2@gmail.com with questions.
After publication of the main paper, focus is shifting to the spin-off projects: 

MB3T studying test-retest reliability is in the final stages of analysis and getting ready for the final write-up.

MB3L studying a longitudinal follow-up of language learning related to rule-learning is wrapping up data collection and getting ready for data-analysis.

MB3P studying pupil size effects in rule-learning is in the midst of organising and pre-processing the eye-tracking data.
MB4I spin-off: Cross-cultural findings on infant prosocial behaviors, caregiver prosocial values and beliefs, and early socialization practices were presented at 2026 BTSCON. The recording can be found here.
The data dictionary, templates for data files, and MB5 data validator are now available! You can find links to these resources in the MB5 Lab Manual.
We had two Design Update meetings earlier this week, where we introduced working subgroups. The meeting recordings will be shared via the MB7 listserv, and contributors can indicate their interest in joining any of the subgroups here. The next update meeting will take place on November 30, 5pm CET.
Preparations for data collection are underway. If any new sites are interested in joining, please reach out!
Our first collective meeting with members of each working group will meet November 5.
