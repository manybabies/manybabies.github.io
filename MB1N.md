<p><img src="/assets/img/MB1Nmap.png" height="250" style="float: right; margin-left:25px; margin-top:30px; margin-bottom: 50px; filter: drop-shadow(5px 5px 3px #7F7F7F);"> All infants from <a href="{{site.baseurl}}/MB1/"><b>MB1</b></a>, regardless of their linguistic background, heard stimuli recorded in North American English. In this ongoing project we ask how does Infant Directed Speech (IDS) preference to North American English in MB1 compare to IDS preference measured in non-English speaking babies’ native language. Infants who are primarily exposed to either French, Dutch, Norwegian, Czech, or Turkish will be tested in their native languages using the same procedure from MB1.</p>
